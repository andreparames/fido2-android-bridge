## Scenario

You manage infrastructure for dozens of small business clients. Each client has servers, admin panels, and dashboards that should be behind stronger-than-password sign-in. Hardware keys do not scale across client environments, and running a different identity stack per client is operationally painful.

## How Gatebridge helps

- Your technicians pair one phone; it works across all client servers.
- The Team plan admin console gives you centralized device management and audit logs.
- Per-team access lists keep client credentials separate.
- Reseller and white-label options are on the roadmap for larger deployments.

## Typical setup

```
MSP technician → Client bastion A ─┐
                                  ├─→ Technician's phone (one phone, many apps)
MSP technician → Client bastion B ─┘
```
